About Us

The story behind Prolinker

Why this exists

Prolinker started as a simple idea: the early days of motherhood are full of questions nobody quite prepares you for. Breastfeeding, sleepless nights, and the hundred small decisions in between — we wanted a calm, honest place to talk about all of it.

What you'll find here

Practical guides, gentle encouragement, and real talk about the parts of new motherhood that don't usually make it into the parenting books. No judgment, no unrealistic expectations — just support for wherever you are in the journey.

Our approach

Every post here is written to be read in the middle of a long night or a short nap window — short on fluff, long on things you can actually use. We lean on real experience and widely accepted guidance, and we always tell you when it's time to loop in a professional instead of a blog post.

Who's behind it

Prolinker is written and maintained independently, without corporate backing or sponsorship pressure. That means the advice here answers to you, not to an advertiser's agenda.

A quick note

Nothing on this site replaces advice from your doctor, midwife, or a certified lactation consultant. Think of this as a friendly starting point, not a medical authority.
